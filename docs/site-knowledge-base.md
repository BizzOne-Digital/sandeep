### Booking & Scheduling
**Q: How do I book a cleaning?**

**Q: What are your business hours?**  
A: Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

**Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?**  
A: Yes! Recurring clients enjoy flexible rescheduling. Contact us as soon as possible to adjust your appointment.

**Q: Do you offer same-day or emergency cleaning?**  
A: Contact us directly — we'll do our best to accommodate urgent requests based on team availability.

### Services & Pricing
**Q: How much does home cleaning cost?**  
A: Recurring home cleaning starts at $99 per visit. Deep cleans start at $249. Final pricing depends on home size, frequency, and add-ons.

**Q: Do you bring your own supplies?**  
A: Yes! All eco-friendly cleaning products and equipment are included in our service.

**Q: Do you offer commercial cleaning quotes?**  
A: Yes. Commercial and specialty services are custom-quoted. We offer free on-site consultations for offices and retail spaces.

**Q: What's included in a deep clean vs. recurring clean?**  
A: Recurring cleans maintain your home with kitchen, bathroom, dusting, vacuuming, and mopping. Deep cleans add baseboards, inside appliances, detailed scrubbing, and areas typically missed in regular maintenance.

### Eco & Safety
**Q: Are your products safe for pets and children?**  
A: Absolutely. We use 100% plant-based products that are safe for families, pets, and allergy-sensitive individuals.

**Q: What is a Green Deep Clean?**  
A: Our Green Deep Clean uses exclusively plant-based products and HEPA vacuuming for a thorough clean that's ideal for allergy sufferers and eco-conscious households.

### Service Area
**Q: What areas do you serve?**  
A: We primarily serve Edmonton, St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Leduc, and Spruce Grove in Alberta. We also serve the Greater Toronto Area for select commercial projects.

**Q: Is there a travel fee?**

### Payment & Policies

A: Yes! Get your free quote through our contact form or by calling +1 416-710-5808.

**Q: What is your satisfaction guarantee?**
